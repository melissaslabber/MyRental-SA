MyRental SA privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

MyRental SA provides tools for private landlords to organise rental properties, tenant records, inspections and property finances.

Information processed

We process account names, email addresses, contact details and sign-in information; property addresses and rental terms; tenant information that landlords enter; inspection notes and photographs; financial entries, municipal statements, invoices and receipts; subscription and payment references; account activity and support/deletion requests.

Purpose and service providers

Information is used to operate and secure accounts, store rental records, provide reports, verify subscriptions and respond to requests. Supabase provides authentication, database and file storage. Vercel hosts the website. PayFast processes website subscription payments. When configured, Resend delivers account/payment emails. Card details are entered with PayFast and are not stored by MyRental SA. Report downloads and sharing send information only to destinations you select.

Photos and documents

You choose which photos and files to upload. Financial supporting documents are stored privately and opened through temporary signed links. The Android app uses system file selection, sharing and printing. It does not request contacts, advertising identifiers, background location or access to all files.

Optional agent contact requests

If you ask a Pam Golding rental agent to contact you, we record your name, surname, contact details, property address, optional note and consent. MyRental SA uses these details to arrange the requested contact and, with your permission, shares them with the relevant Pam Golding rental agent. Enquiries are retained for follow-up and support; you may request deletion through the account/data deletion route below.

Security and access

Network communication uses HTTPS. Account records are separated by owner access rules. Authorised administrators can access account and subscription information for support and administration. Never upload information you have no authority to process, and keep your login secure.

Retention and deletion

Records remain while needed to provide the service and meet lawful retention duties. Cancellation alone stops membership access; it does not delete the account. You can request account and associated-data deletion in the app under Settings or through the web route below. Requests are reviewed to verify ownership, stop outstanding billing and remove associated data, except information that must be retained for legal, accounting or fraud-prevention purposes. The deletion request is not confirmation of completed deletion.

Your requests

You can update profile details in Settings, export financial reports, and initiate account/data deletion using the secure request form. If you cannot sign in, use password reset first so your identity can be verified.
