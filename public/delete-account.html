Delete your MyRental SA account and associated data

You can initiate deletion without reinstalling the Android app.

  1. Sign in using the secure link below. Use “Forgot password?” if needed.
  2. Read the explanation, confirm your request and submit it.
  3. Your request is recorded for the administrator to verify and process. Pending payment/cancellation issues must be checked before deletion is completed.

Sign in and request deletion

What happens to your information?

Account details, property records, tenant information, uploaded files and associated rental data are included in the deletion request. Certain payment, accounting, security or legal records may need to be retained. Request submission does not mean that data has already been deleted.

You can also initiate deletion inside the app under Settings → Delete account and associated data. Subscription cancellation alone does not delete your account.
