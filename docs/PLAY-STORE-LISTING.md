# Store listing draft

App name: **MyRental SA**

Short description: **Manage rentals, invoices, inspections and property finances in one place.**

## Full description

MyRental SA helps South African private landlords keep their rental administration organised.

Keep properties and lease details together. Prepare monthly rental invoices with rent and agreed services such as water, electricity, refuse, Wi-Fi and garden maintenance. Share professional PDF documents, record full or partial payments, and see outstanding balances clearly.

Record municipal accounts, repairs and other property expenses with private supporting documents. Prepare March-to-February income and expense reports to review with your accountant. Tenant reimbursements and bills paid directly by tenants are shown separately.

Use inspection records, maintenance tracking, rental guidance and clear next-step checklists. Friendly payment reminders and breach-notice drafts help you organise communication and keep delivery records. Legal guidance does not replace professional advice, and notices must be checked against the lease and applicable law.

The Android app is for existing MyRental SA account holders. Features requiring cloud storage need an internet connection.
